Recruiting

I am looking for Ph.D. students, MPhil students, Interns and RAs (from Summer/Fall 2026) to join my lab. If you are interested in computer architecture research at CUHK, see the guidelines below.

I am broadly interested in architecture, microarchitecture, compilers and programming languages to support demanding applications on next generation hardware. Constraints on technology are pushing architects to develop customized hardware for different application scenarios, and there is growing consensus that significant degrees of heterogeneity are necessary. Much of the burden of improving computer systems will be on computer architects, not only in designing new hardware, but also in figuring out how to rapidly codesign the many components of systems (eg. compilers and architecture). This is exciting for architects because our task is critical and extremely challenging.

My research requires a variety of different skills and expertise, including:

Strong general coding skills (C, C++, Python, …)
Hardware Simulators and Modeling
Compiler Implementation (LLVM, clang, GCC…)
Application Development (databases, machine learning, computer vision…)
Hardware design and prototyping (Verilog, Synthesis, FPGA experience…)

Guidelines

Drop me an email (zhengrongwang AT cuhk.edu.hk) with the subject “Apply-{PhD|MPhil|RA|Intern}-{Your Name}” (e.g., PhD-Trump). Please include your CV and transcripts in the email. If you are on campus, feel free to drop by my office during student consultation time (Wed. 3-5 pm).

Research: You want to do solid research that has real-world impact. Publication: It’s a plus but not necessary. Programming: Strong programming skills. Open-Source: If you have open-sourced projects, you’re welcomed to indicate that in your email/CV.

The details of application process on MPhil/PhD can be found here. The regular admission for 2026 intake has three deadlines, 9 Oct 2025, 15 Jan 2026, and 31 Mar 2026.

All Ph.D. students will be offered with a full-time scholarship (~18k HKD/month). Exceptional applicants will be recommended to apply for HKPFS (~28k HKD/month) or Vice-Chancellor scholarship. Scholarship details can be found here.
