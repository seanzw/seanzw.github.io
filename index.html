About Me

I am currently an assistant professor at CSE Department, The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I am generally interested in developing high performance computer architecture for both general and domain specific computing with compiler support. I am also looking into how to accurately and efficiently model and simulate current architectures.

I obtained my Ph.D. from UCLA, working with Prof. Tony Nowatzki. Prior joining UCLA, I received Bachelor of Engineering from Department of Electronic Enginerring, Tsinghua University. I also studied at ETH Zürich for half a year as a exhange student.

In my spare time, I enjoy playing badminton, rock climbing, running and guitar.

Email: zhengrongwang[AT]cuhk.edu.hk
