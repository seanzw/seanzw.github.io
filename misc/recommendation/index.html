I am glad to write recommendation letters for students I know well.

  • You have worked with me on serious research for at least one semester or one summer; OR
  • You have taken at least one course from me, received the grade of A with active participation.

What I need from you

To help me write a concrete and detailed letter, please send me the following materials (preferably in a single PDF or a zip file):

  • Your transcript (including ranking information, if available)
  • The score(s) you received in my course(s) and/or research project(s)
  • Your project report (if applicable)
  • Your resume / CV
  • Your statement of purpose (draft is fine)
  • A list of universities you are applying to, along with their deadlines (so I can track submissions)
  • Any other supporting documents (e.g., TOEFL/GRE score reports, certificates)

Additional notes

  • I will write at most 10 letters per student per application cycle.
  • In online submission systems, please fill in as many fields as possible before sending me the request link.
  • I appreciate your understanding and respect for my time.
